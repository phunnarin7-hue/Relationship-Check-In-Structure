✨ Step 1: Appreciation (5-10 minutes)

Begin together with warmth. Each person shares:

One thing I appreciated about you this week
One thing you did well
One thing I'm grateful for
⏱️ Set a gentle timer: 5-10 minutes total

💬 Step 2: Check-in (15-30 minutes)

Take turns sharing without interruption. Remember the rules as you listen.

How have I been feeling lately?
What felt difficult this week?
Is there anything unresolved?
Listen fully before responding. The goal is understanding, not solving. If either person feels overwhelmed, it's OK to pause—you can come back later.

🤔 Step 3: Reflection (a few days)

After the check-in, give yourselves space to think.

Process your emotions on your own
Think about possible solutions
Let understanding deepen
No pressure for immediate answers. Give it a few days.

🤝 Step 4: Solution Conversation

Later in the week or next weekend, come back together with fresh perspective.

Discuss possible solutions together
Explore compromises
Decide on small actions you can both take
Offer support to each other
Remember: the relationship is the problem, not each other. You're working as a team.

The 7 Rules for Every Check-in

1
We listen fully before responding
2
No interrupting
3
No sarcasm or attacking each other
4
Speak from feelings, not blame ("I felt…" instead of "You always…")
5
We are not trying to win, we are trying to understand each other
6
Pause is allowed if someone feels overwhelmed, but we come back later
7
The relationship is the problem, not each other

The core idea: Separate check-in from problem-solving so you can stay emotionally grounded and not become defensive. This structure creates safety and understanding first.
